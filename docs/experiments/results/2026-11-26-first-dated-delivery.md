## What this does and does not establish

This record connects an ordinary task to a dated registration, first delivery, independently implemented check, and non-producer adjudication. It closes a concrete engineering gap exposed by the [earlier simulated diagnosis](2026-11-26-decision-information-limits.md), where positive verification claims accompanied invalid artifacts. The two exercises have different tasks and evidence; their scores are not a before-and-after performance comparison.

Target-file existence is narrower than a working rendered link. Heading fragments, rendered navigation, semantic suitability, external URLs and the scientific truth of the document remain unmeasured. There was no missing local target in this selected document, so this delivery does not supply real-case evidence for detecting one.

Quality, authority and time assurance remain separate. The recorded local producer submission permission was effective. Separate verifier and adjudicator authority evidence was absent, so those authority fields remained unknown despite the quality pass. Actor names express local role attribution. No independent timestamp witness or external identity proof was recorded. Hashes establish consistency with retained bytes, not historical truth by themselves.
