This was one [registered exploratory comparison](../control-exchange-fresh-comparison.md): 24 new scripted cases, two compact record methods, and two lossless controls. The two compact methods agreed on five successful releases. Chronology alone succeeded on two cases; the relational method alone succeeded on two others. Neither succeeded on the remaining fifteen. The net difference was zero. These paired counts matter: equal totals conceal different failures.

All three invalid-artifact proposals became accepted simulated releases in every arm. The guard checks authority and earlier releases; it does not establish delivery quality. The observed verification records said pass, while the evaluator's separately stipulated later outcomes said the artifacts were invalid. Zero unauthorized or duplicate effects therefore cannot be read as a general safety result.

Those invalid outcomes were deliberately included in the case design. Their frequency does not estimate the prevalence of verification failures in a real service. The simulation also does not establish how a real verifier would obtain or validate the underlying evidence. It makes that missing assurance visible.

There were eleven valid opportunities in the simulated world, but only nine were actionable from the evidence available at the cutoff. Lossless records recovered the storage losses and still could not complete the other two. Late revocation, a late dispute and a late prior release also produced the same harmful proposals in every arm. A complete local record is complete only with respect to the information actually observed.

The registered rule required at least two additional valid timely releases, no increase in each proposal-harm count, no additional failed outputs and passing conformance before preparing a prospective test. The zero net difference did not meet that rule. The checked result selects **investigation of information limits**, retaining the chronological baseline. The next question is which decision-relevant facts were lost, unavailable, or contradicted by the later outcome. These exposed cases will not be retuned or rerun to manufacture an improvement.

All six program checks passed. Parent computation and attempt journaling measured 5.823 seconds; the enclosing process measured 6.120 seconds through final caller persistence. These timings overlap. Programs made no model calls, incurred no external spend and performed no real payment. Agent/reviewer work and memory costs remain unmeasured. The eight decision-related test modules passed 158 tests. A separate checker verified all 96 attempts, 569 source spans and 392 aggregate/stratum ratios without importing the engine or executing a candidate. The actual controller accepted that retained evidence and atomically queued the information-limit investigation.
