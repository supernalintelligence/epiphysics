       5. CRITICAL GAPS AND TENSIONS

       Internal Consistency Gaps:

       1. Measurement gap is conceded but unresolved: Carnot ceiling, optimal viscosity, belief state inertia, consciousness tensor all require operationalization. The framework is "grammar awaiting vocabulary" (efficiency_limits.md
       Section 7) — acknowledged throughout but no concrete path forward is specified.
       2. Relabeling vs. Novel distinction sometimes blurred: Many "structural" claims (e.g., duplication waste scaling as N²) are restatements of known combinatorics. The framework's value is claimed to be in unified classification but
       this itself may be relabeling.
       3. Two distinct audiences, one framework: Physics audience (causal sets, U(1) derivation) and applications audience (org efficiency, AI alignment) are separated by a large conceptual gap. The ELEVATOR_PITCH frames it as "one grammar"
       but the papers show: physics theory (Parts 0–5, amplitude/gauge work) is largely self-contained; applications import thermodynamic quantities but don't reverse-validate the framework.
       4. Quasi-static assumption not addressed for real systems: Carnot efficiency requires reversible, quasi-static processes; organizations and AI systems are far-from-equilibrium. The framework admits this (efficiency_limits.md Section
       5.2) but proposes no correction — only hand-waves to Curzon-Ahlborn.
       5. Agent behavior not derived, only constrained: The POMDP framework in Decision and Trajectory is a dual description of the generalized mechanics, not a derivation of choice from physics. The claim is "dual" but the causal arrow
       (does mechanics determine policy, or does policy determine mechanics?) remains ambiguous.

       Prediction Validation Gaps:

       - Efficiency Limits: Testable in principle but requires operationalization of T_org, S_org, F. No pilot studies reported.
       - Companies as Entities: Three novel predictions (genome→M&A, keystones→resilience, coupling→leading indicator) — none tested. Framework is "load-bearing" but untested.
       - Decision and Trajectory: Belief inertia, belief divergence, institutional γ all connect to existing empirical work (Bayesian updating, echo chambers, Turchin) but Epimechanics doesn't generate new predictions beyond reframing.
       - Civilizational: Cliodynamics (Turchin) is cited as evidence but independently developed; not derived from Epimechanics.

       Positioning Vulnerability:

       - If U(1) derivation and gauge symmetry conjecture fail, the physics anchor dissolves — applications are left orphaned.
       - If applications remain untested and operationalization of T, S, C, I, A remains unsolved, Epimechanics is "vocabulary seeking grammar," not grammar seeking vocabulary.
       - Outreach strategy assumes Nature-tier reception; this is high-risk absent empirical validation in at least one domain.
